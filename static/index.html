      What this does and doesn't do

      Passive means public DNS, public certificate-transparency logs, a normal TLS handshake and ordinary page requests, with no exploitation and no password guessing. It focuses on misconfiguration and exposed services, common root causes of incidents for small organisations. It is not a penetration test or a compliance audit.

      Automated results can contain false positives. Review with your IT provider.
